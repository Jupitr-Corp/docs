## Create your account

<Steps>

### Sign up

Go to the [Hikoo app](https://app.tryhikoo.com) and create an account with your email and password, or click **Continue with Google**. New accounts go straight into onboarding.

### Add your website

Enter your website URL and name, and choose the target market you want to track (**Worldwide** or a specific country). Hikoo analyzes the site to prepare your profile.

### Confirm your profile

Review the **Description**, **Industry**, **Brand identity**, and **Topics** Hikoo detected. Topics drive the prompts Hikoo suggests, so accurate topics make every later view easier to slice.

### Add competitors

Review the competitors Hikoo detected from your site, then add, edit, or remove brands. You need at least one competitor, with a name and a URL, to continue. You can change the list later from the [Competitors](/guides/competitors) page.

### Pick prompts

Hikoo suggests prompts (the questions it will ask AI assistants). Select the ones that match how your audience searches, click **Regenerate suggestions** for more ideas, and confirm.

### See your snapshot

Hikoo shows a first snapshot of how AI search talks about your brand, next to your competitors.

### Choose a plan

Click **Start free trial** (no credit card required) or pick a plan and check out. Plans differ by websites, prompts, AI answer runs, platforms, and which features they include.

</Steps>

## What happens next

Once your website is set up, Hikoo works in the background:

- **First scan.** A banner on the dashboard tracks your first AI visibility scan as it generates prompts, queries the AI platforms, and indexes the answers.
- **Answer collection.** Hikoo runs your prompts on the AI assistants every night and records whether they name or link you. See [Citations](/guides/citations).
- **Indexation.** Your site is crawled to discover and categorize its pages. See [Pages](/guides/pages).
- **Health checks.** Your site is evaluated for AI readiness, including `robots.txt` crawler access, `llms.txt`, and sitemap presence. See [Website health](/guides/health).

Data fills in on your dashboard as results arrive.
