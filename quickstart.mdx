## Create your account

<Steps>

### Sign up

Head to the Hikoo app and create an account with your email and password, or sign in with Google.

### Add your website

During onboarding you will be asked to:

1. **Enter your website URL** and company name.
2. **Select your target market** - choose Worldwide or a specific country to focus on.
3. **Describe your website** - tell us what your site is about so we can generate relevant tracking prompts.
4. **Add competitors** (optional) - enter competitor domains you want to benchmark against.

### Choose a plan

Select a subscription plan that fits your needs. Plans differ by:

- Number of websites you can monitor
- Number of prompts per website
- Monthly audit and health-check quotas
- Number of indexed pages

Complete the checkout and you are ready to go.

</Steps>

## What happens next

Once your website is added, Hikoo begins working in the background:

- **Indexation** - your site is crawled to discover and categorize all pages.
- **Citation collection** - we start tracking when AI platforms cite your content.
- **Health checks** - your site is evaluated for AI discoverability signals like `robots.txt` rules, `llms.txt`, and sitemap presence.

You will see data populate on your dashboard as results come in.
