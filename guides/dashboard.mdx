The dashboard is your home screen in Hikoo. It shows a summary of how your website is performing across AI platforms.

## Overview cards

When you open the dashboard you will see:

- **Visibility trend** - a line chart showing how often your site has been cited over time.
- **Competitor ranking** - a ranked list of your site and its competitors by total citation count.
- **Citation share** - a donut chart breaking down what percentage of citations go to you versus competitors.
- **Top sources** - which AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, etc.) cite you most often.

## Filters

Every chart on the dashboard responds to a shared set of filters in the toolbar:

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Time range** | Narrow results to a specific period - all time, 1 year, 1 month, 1 week, or 1 day. |
| **Platform** | Show data for a single AI platform or all platforms. |
| **Localization** | Focus on a geographic market (e.g. US, FR). |

Filters persist as you navigate between pages so you do not need to re-apply them.

## Switching websites

If you monitor more than one website, use the **site switcher** in the sidebar to jump between them. The dashboard and all sub-pages update automatically.
