The dashboard is your home screen. It summarizes how the selected website performs across AI platforms, and it is the starting point for every other module.

## Dashboard and Hiko

A **Dashboard** / **Hiko** switch sits at the top of the sidebar. **Dashboard** shows the pages described in these guides. **Hiko** opens the assistant that answers questions about your data in plain language. See [Hiko](/guides/hiko).

## Key metrics

Three cards open the page. Each shows its change against the comparison period.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Visibility** | The share of AI answers that name your brand. |
| **Share of voice** | Your share of all brand mentions, with your rank among tracked brands. |
| **Citations** | The share of answers that show a link to your site. |

## Charts and rankings

- **Visibility trend.** The share of answers naming you and your competitors over time. Switch the metric to **Average position** to see where each brand is named in the answers.
- **Competitors.** Your brand ranked against competitors by visibility. When Hikoo spots new competitors in your answers, a banner invites you to **Review** them.
- **Top cited sources.** The domains AI engines rely on to answer your prompts. Toggle between **Domains** and **URLs**.
- **Share of voice.** Your share of brand mentions over time, against your strongest rival.
- **Top AI platforms.** The share of each platform's answers that name you.

Markers on the trend charts show what changed on your site, such as prompts added or archived, competitors edited, or a plan change. Click a marker to see the events for that day and to add your own note, for example "pricing page launched".

## First scan banner

For a brand-new website, a banner tracks your first AI visibility scan as it moves through **Generating prompts**, **Querying AI platforms**, and **Indexing answers**. Data fills in as each stage completes. If the site has no prompts yet, a banner invites you to add some.

## The filter bar

A filter bar at the top drives every card. The same bar appears on Citations, Prompts, and the other analysis pages, and your selection follows you as you navigate.

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Period** | **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 12 months**, **All time**, or a custom range picked on the calendar. |
| **Compare to** | The period the changes are measured against: **Previous period**, **Same period last year**, **Another period**, or **None**. |
| **Platforms** | One or more AI platforms. |
| **Topics** | One or more [topics](/guides/topics). Shown once the site has topics. |
| **Markets** | One or more countries, or **Worldwide**. |
| **Brand scope** | **Branded** prompts (they name your brand) or **Non-branded** prompts. |
| **Intent** | The search intent of the prompts: informational, comparative, transactional, or navigational. |
| **Tags** | One or more prompt [tags](/guides/prompts#tags). Shown once the site has tags. |

**Brand scope** and **Platforms** stay on the bar. Click **Filter** to add the others. **Clear all** removes every filter but keeps the period.

## Switching websites

If you track more than one website, use the **site switcher** in the sidebar to jump between them. Sites are grouped by workspace, and the whole dashboard updates to the site you pick. Sites without an active subscription prompt you to choose a plan first.
