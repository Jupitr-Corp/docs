The **Decisions** screen is where you answer everything Hikoo brings you. Open it from the Outreach home with **Start deciding**, or from a card in **Needs you**. Each decision is one card, with Hikoo's pick highlighted and the message it would send.

What reaches this screen depends on your [permission grid](/guides/outreach/settings#who-does-what).

## The decision queue

The queue on the side shows where you are, for example **3 of 12, about 6 min**. Cards are grouped by urgency:

| Group | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Deadlines** | Legal deadlines, such as a personal data request. |
| **Changed terms** | A site changed the terms you agreed on. |
| **Their asks** | A site asks for something in return, such as a sample, a trial, or a link back. |
| **Hikoo needs you** | Hikoo needs an answer to go on. |
| **Where is it?** | A mention to check. |
| **Replies waiting to go out** | Replies Hikoo wrote and holds for your OK. |
| **First emails to approve** | First emails Hikoo wrote and holds for your OK. |
| **Ready to send** | Mails ready to leave. |

Each card says **Why it waits for you**, shows the context of the conversation, and lists the choices. Click **Preview the message** to read what a choice would send. Some choices send nothing.

### Keyboard shortcuts

- **Enter** follows Hikoo's pick.
- **1** to **3** pick the other choices.
- **E** edits the mail shown.
- **Escape** steps back.

After a decision, a toast lets you **Undo** it. Undo is no longer possible once the mail has left.

## Approve a first email

When **Write the first email** or **Send the first email** asks you first, every first email waits here. The card reads **Hikoo wrote a first email to** the contact at the site.

- Click **Approve and send**. The email leaves from your mailbox inside your sending hours.
- Click **Edit before sending** to change the text yourself, then **Send my version**. A version that breaks a sending rule is refused and nothing is sent.
- Click **Tell Hikoo what to change** and describe the change, for example "shorter, and thank them for the quick answer". Hikoo rewrites the email.
- Click **Open the conversation** to see the whole opportunity.

If the brand description changed since a first email was written, the card says so and offers **Rewrite this mail**.

### Versions

Each rewrite keeps the previous text. Use the arrows next to **Version 2 of 3** to move between versions, and **Show changes** to see what was added and removed. The version you leave selected is the one that would be sent.

## Replies and follow-ups

Hikoo writes replies and follow-ups too. When the matching square of the grid asks you first, the card reads **Hikoo wrote a reply to** or **Hikoo wrote a follow-up to** the contact. You see what they wrote next to **Hikoo's answer**, and you approve, edit, or rewrite it the same way.

### Positive replies

A positive reply is a reply where the site is ready to talk. The **Answer a positive reply** square of the grid decides whether Hikoo answers it alone, answers and tells you after, or asks you first.

## Other decisions

Cards also cover:

- **Changed terms** after you agreed, or after publication.
- **Their asks**, such as a sample, a loan, a discount code, a visit, a trial, a free account, a social share, an affiliate link, or a link back.
- A call request: offer times for a call, or keep it to email.
- A personal data request, with its legal deadline.
- A reply in another language.
- **Text to approve**, when a site asks you to validate the copy.
- A new contact, when the person you wrote to points you to someone else.
- Whether the mention is live, when Hikoo cannot read the page.

When every card is answered, you see **Hikoo takes it from here** and Hikoo carries on until it needs you again.
