Open **Outreach**, then **Settings**. Every change saves as you make it. There is no **Save** button.

<Note>
Only the workspace owner and admins change these settings. Members read them, take decisions, and approve emails from the Outreach home.
</Note>

## Sending mailboxes

Hikoo sends from your own address. Every member sends from their own mailbox, and replies land in that inbox. Viewers send nothing, so they have no mailbox to connect.

<Steps>
  ### Connect your mailbox
  Click **Gmail** or **Outlook** and sign in. Leave the permission to send email ticked, or nothing is stored. Hikoo connects to Gmail and Outlook for now.

  ### Add it to the site
  Under the site's sending mailboxes, click **Add to this site**. The site's emails leave only from the mailboxes attached to it.

  ### Check its state
  Each mailbox shows whether it **Sends for this site**, is **Over the plan, does not send here**, has a **Connection expired**, or had its **Sending stopped**.
</Steps>

The site's plan sets how many mailboxes can send for it. One mailbox per person: a member who connects theirs from this site takes a slot.

Each mailbox has a daily sending pace. A new mailbox starts **Warming up** with a lower daily cap. Sending stops for a while if there are too many bounces or spam refusals, or when the provider's daily limit is reached.

To remove a mailbox from a site, click **Remove**. The first emails it carried move to another mailbox of the site, and its ongoing conversations wait for you. It keeps sending for its other sites.

### Signature

Your signature is set with your mailbox in your account **Settings**, under **Profile**. It is added as is under every email you send: name, role, company, phone, link. Hikoo never writes it. Left empty, the email goes out with your name.

<Warning>
If a mailbox connection expires, nothing goes out from it and replies are not read until you click **Reconnect**.
</Warning>

## What we are

Hikoo writes every email from the description of your site. You edit it in the site's settings with **Edit in the site's settings**. See [Websites](/guides/websites).

## How much Hikoo does alone

Pick a preset to fill the whole permission grid at once:

| Preset | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Asks you everything** | You approve every mail before it leaves: first emails, replies, and follow-ups. |
| **Follows your rules** | Hikoo writes, answers, and follows up on its own. It comes to you for legal questions and anything unusual. |
| **Runs on its own** | Hikoo goes all the way to the mention. You only see the exceptions and the wins. |
| **Custom** | Your own combination, shown when you move a square yourself. |

When you raise a level, Hikoo asks what to do with the first emails and follow-ups already waiting for your OK. Click **Send it** or **Send all** to release them inside your sending hours, or **Keep it** or **Keep them** to approve them one by one.

## Who does what

The permission grid sets one level for each kind of work. The higher the block, the more Hikoo involves you. Click a block to move it.

**Levels:**

- **Asks you first**: Hikoo prepares the work and waits for your OK on the [decisions](/guides/outreach/decisions) screen.
- **Does it, then tells you**: Hikoo goes ahead, and you see it on the Outreach home the next time you come.
- **Does it alone**: Hikoo goes ahead without telling you. It still appears in **Activity**.

**Kinds of work:**

| Kind of work | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Find opportunities** | Spot pages and sites worth getting mentioned on. Always Hikoo: looking is what Hikoo is for, and this one cannot be changed. |
| **Qualify the opportunity** | Decide whether a source is worth pursuing for your brand. |
| **Find the right person** | Find the named person who can change the page, such as the author or the editor. |
| **Research that person** | Read what that person publishes so the first email speaks to them. |
| **Write the first email** | Draft the pitch for that person. |
| **Send the first email** | Send the pitch from your connected mailbox. |
| **Send a follow-up** | Send the next email when the person has not answered. Hikoo reads this setting for every mail it writes inside a conversation. |
| **Answer a positive reply** | Reply when the person says yes or asks for details. Read for every mail inside a conversation. |
| **Answer an unusual objection** | Reply to a question or a refusal outside the usual cases. Read for every mail inside a conversation. |

A sentence under the grid sums it up, for example how many kinds of work Hikoo asks you first for.

## Follow-ups

- **Follow-ups at most, per opportunity**: how many times Hikoo comes back when nobody answers. Three at most. Set it to `0` to turn follow-ups off.
- **Days of silence before the next one**: how long Hikoo waits.

Whether a follow-up leaves on its own or waits for you is set by the **Send a follow-up** square of the grid.

## People with no address

Sometimes Hikoo finds the right person and no way to email them. Turn on **Hand over their LinkedIn profile** and Hikoo writes the note and gives you the profile to paste. Hikoo does not send on LinkedIn: it holds no account there.

Turn it off for email only. Those opportunities are treated like any other with no usable address: Hikoo keeps looking, tries the address the site publishes, and closes the opportunity if there is none.

## Documents and resources

Add what Hikoo can offer a publisher: an affiliate program, a partner deck, a case study, or the brand's visuals.

1. Click **Add a file** or **Add a link**. Files can be PDF, Word, PNG, JPEG, or WebP, 10 MB max.
2. Hikoo reads it and prepares the entry: name, summary, and **When to offer it**.
3. Check what Hikoo read, correct it if needed, and click **Save**.

Resources are always shared as a link, never as an attachment. Use **Move to the media kit** to file visuals together. Deleting a file stops the links already shared with publishers.

## Offer and sender profile

Two more settings are available through the REST API:

- **What the brand offers**: the things the brand can give a publisher who says yes, such as a spokesperson, data, a product to test, imagery, or a discount or trial. Whatever is not listed is not offered. Left empty, Hikoo offers a description of the brand and a link, nothing else.
- **Sender profile**: how the people in your mailboxes stand toward the brand. They can be an employee, an agency working for the brand (with the agency's name), or a consultant writing on its behalf. Left empty, Hikoo decides per mailbox: "works at the brand" when the mailbox is on one of the brand's domains, "writing on behalf of" otherwise.

## Do-not-contact list

The workspace keeps one do-not-contact list, shared by all its sites. An entry blocks a single address or a whole domain. Adding an entry cancels any draft already queued for it.

Entries are added when you close an opportunity with **Do not contact**. Choose **Everyone at** the domain or the person only. You can also list, add, and remove entries through the REST API.
