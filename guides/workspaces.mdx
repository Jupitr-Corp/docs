title: "Workspaces"
description: "Organize websites and teammates under a workspace with its own roles and subscription."
---

A workspace is a team container. It holds websites and a subscription, and it is where you invite teammates and manage their access. You can belong to several workspaces at once, and the site switcher groups your websites by the workspace they live in.

## Roles

Every member of a workspace has a role that sets what they can do.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Full control, including the plan, settings, and transferring ownership. |
| Admin | Manage the plan, settings, sites, and members. |
| Member | Work with sites and data, without managing the plan. |
| Viewer | Read-only access. |

A Viewer can see the workspace but cannot make changes. Write actions are blocked, with a tooltip explaining why and a toast if they try. Owners and Admins are the roles that manage the plan and workspace settings.

## Manage members

Open **Settings**, then **Members**. From there you can:

- Click **Invite** to invite a teammate by email and assign a role.
- See **Pending Invitations** and click **Revoke invitation** on one that has not been accepted.
- **Promote to admin** or **Demote to member**.
- **Remove** a member from the workspace.
- **Transfer ownership** to another member.

<Note>Transferring ownership makes the other member the owner and makes you an admin. You confirm by typing their name, and you cannot undo it without their cooperation.</Note>

## Where workspaces are managed

Workspaces are managed from the Settings modal, which opens from the user menu. Pick the workspace in the switcher at the top of the **Workspace** group, then open a section:

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sites** | List the sites in the workspace, add a site, and choose or change each site's plan. |
| **Integrations** | Connect Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Shopify, and Vercel. |
| **Members** | Invite, review, and manage the people in the workspace and their roles. |
| **Billing** | Owners and admins only. Review per-site subscription status, open the billing portal, and view invoices. |
| **Settings** | Rename the workspace, require two-factor authentication, and delete or leave the workspace. |

<Info>Billing in a workspace is per site. The **Billing** section shows how many sites have an active subscription out of the total, so you can see coverage at a glance.</Info>
