Hikoo exposes a **Model Context Protocol (MCP) server** so you can ask Claude (or any MCP-compatible client) about your GEO data in plain language. Citations, audits, prompts, competitors, and more, all without leaving your chat.

Ask things like:

- "How is my site cited compared to competitors this month?"
- "List the pages that failed criteria in my latest audit."
- "Add a prompt about pricing transparency under the pricing topic."

Claude calls the Hikoo MCP server behind the scenes, which fetches the data from your Hikoo workspace.

## What you get

- **Citation analytics** - ask about visibility trends, top AI platforms, source domains.
- **Audits** - list past audits, inspect page-level criteria results, queue a new audit.
- **Prompts management** - create, update, delete prompts and review their citations.
- **Competitors** - share-of-voice ranking on demand.
- **Source intelligence** - AI-cited domains (review platforms, directories, blogs, media) ranked by frequency, so an agent can automate listing claims and outreach.
- **Website health** - robots, llms.txt, sitemap status, and improvement suggestions.

## Who should use it

- **Power users** comfortable in Claude Desktop, Claude Code, or any MCP client.
- **Analysts** who prefer ad-hoc questions over dashboards for exploration.
- **Teams** building AI workflows that need programmatic access to their GEO data.
