**Indexed Pages** is part of the **Analyzer** module. It is where you index your website and review the inventory of pages Hikoo knows. Hikoo discovers your pages from your sitemap, reads them, and analyzes each one.

For every analyzed page, Hikoo:

- Detects its category, such as home page, blog post, product page, pricing page, documentation page, or FAQ page.
- Detects its language.
- Detects E-E-A-T signals, covering experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness.

## Index your pages

- Click **Start Indexation** to have Hikoo discover and index your pages. A banner shows the progress while it runs, then how many pages were indexed.
- Click **Add a page** to index or re-index a single URL. The URL must belong to your website's domain.

<Note>
The number of pages Hikoo can index depends on your plan.
</Note>

## Overview

Above the table, a summary shows the number of **Indexed pages** and the last indexation date, the main **Page types**, and the main **Languages** of your site.

## The pages table

Each row is one page. The table shows:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Page** | The page address. |
| **Category** | The detected page type. |
| **Lang** | The detected language. |
| **Status** | Where the page is in the pipeline, and its errors or warnings. |
| **Indexed** | When Hikoo last indexed the page. |

A page moves through these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not crawled yet** | Found in your sitemap, but not read yet. The next indexation reads it. |
| **Crawled** | Content read, but not analyzed yet. Its category and language are guessed from the URL for now. |
| **Analyzing…** | Analysis in progress. |
| **Analyzed** | Fully analyzed, with no issue. |
| **Warnings** or **Errors** | Analyzed, with issues to review. |
| **Blocked** | The crawler could not read the page. |

A **WAF blocked** badge means a firewall served a verification page or refused the crawler. AI engines likely hit the same wall. Contact [support@tryhikoo.com](mailto:support@tryhikoo.com) to move forward.

### Filters

Narrow the table with **Search pages**, **Status**, **Category**, and **Language**.

### Row actions

Open the actions menu of a row to:

- **Open page** on your site.
- **Change category** when the detected category is wrong.
- **Delete** a page you do not want tracked.

## Google and Bing data

When you connect Google Search Console or Bing Webmaster Tools, the table adds a **Google** and a **Bing** column with the impressions and clicks of each page. Pick the period with the time range filter. A summary above the table gives the totals for that period.

- Sort the table by impressions or clicks for either tool.
- Google data arrives 2 to 3 days late.
- Bing reports by week, so the period counts whole weeks.

If Hikoo loses access to a tool, a banner asks you to **Reconnect** the account.

## API and MCP

You can read the indexation summary and status, list pages and errors, and re-crawl a URL from the REST API and the [MCP server](/guides/mcp/overview).
