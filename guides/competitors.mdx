**Competitors** is the first page of the **Battlemap** module. It shows how your AI visibility compares to the brands you track, so you can see where you lead, where you are behind, and where to act next.

Everything on this page respects the shared filter bar. Change the time range, platform, or market and every number, chart, and table updates to match.

## What the page shows

The page stacks several cards. Click a brand in any of them to open its profile.

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Top movers** | Who gained or lost the most visibility in the period, split into **Risers** and **Fallers**. Brands that just appeared carry a **New** tag. A **Your trajectory** line shows whether you are trending up, down, or stable. |
| **Share of voice** | A donut that splits AI mentions between you and your tracked competitors. A headline states whether you lead or how far you are behind the leader. |
| **Lost ground** | Topics where a competitor is ahead of you in AI citations. Open a topic to get an action plan. |
| **Visibility trend** | Your visibility over time next to your top competitors. |
| **Competitor ranking** | Every tracked brand, yours included, ranked by visibility. A **Low sample** label warns you when too few answers exist for the percentages to be reliable. |

<Info>
Share of voice is your share of all mentions recorded across you and your competitors. Read more in [Citations](/guides/citations).
</Info>

## Managing competitors

You can add competitors during onboarding or at any time from this page.

<Steps>
  ### Open the manager
  Click **Manage Competitors**. The dialog lists **Your Competitors** with how many you track out of your plan limit.

  ### Add a competitor
  Click **Add Competitor**, then enter the **Name** and **Website URL**. Hikoo starts tracking their mentions alongside yours.

  ### Add aliases and other domains
  Edit a competitor to add **Alternative names**, the other names a brand goes by, and **Other domains**, such as `acme.fr` next to `acme.com`. Mentions and sources that use them still count toward the right competitor.

  ### Remove a competitor
  Delete a competitor you no longer want to track. It leaves your rankings and charts, and frees a slot.
</Steps>

Your plan sets how many competitors you can track. If you track more than your plan allows, the extra ones appear as locked competitors. Their citations are still collected every day, and their analytics unlock on a higher plan.

## Competitor suggestions

Hikoo reads your AI answers and detects brands that keep appearing next to you but that you do not track yet. When it finds some, a banner on **Competitors** says how many were detected, and a badge on **Competitors** in the sidebar shows how many are waiting for review. Click **Review** to open the suggestions page.

Each suggestion shows:

- Its **Mention rate**, the share of your AI answers that name the brand over your whole tracked history.
- When it was first and **Last seen**.

Open a suggestion to see why it was flagged before you decide. The panel shows its average position, the platforms that name it, a trend against the previous period, **Where it comes back** (the prompts whose answers name it most), and **Answers naming it**, the actual AI answers you can page through.

Then pick an action:

- **Promote** a suggestion to track it. Choose **New competitor** to add it as a full competitor, with its URL and alternative names. Past answers that name it are linked automatically.
- Choose **Alternative name of existing** to attach the brand to a competitor you already track. This uses no extra slot, so it still works when you reach your competitor limit.
- **Dismiss** a suggestion to stop seeing it.

Suggestions refresh continuously.

<Note>
Competitor suggestions are available on plans that include them. On other plans, the suggestions area invites you to upgrade.
</Note>

## Competitor profile

Click any brand to open its profile in a side panel.

- KPI tiles for **Mentions**, **Visibility**, and **Avg position**.
- A short summary that explains the brand's position in plain words.
- **Visibility over time** for the selected period.
- **Performance by topic**, the brand's rank against yours on each active topic.
- **Sources citing them**, the places where AI assistants cite this brand, ranked by source type.
- **Recent answers** that name the brand, with a link to see all of them.
- **Linked social networks**, the creators and contents tied to this brand on social networks.

## Lost ground topics

Open a topic from the **Lost ground** card to get a guided action plan for it. The page answers three questions in order.

<Steps>
  ### Where you're losing
  See who out-cites you on this topic, by how much, and the prompts involved.

  ### Fix it
  Get one move to earn a citation here. Hikoo lists the **Pages to win**, the pages where the leader earns its citations, starting with the best one. Affiliate and sponsored pages are flagged, and pages that already list you are shown apart so you can push for a better placement instead of pitching. Use **Copy brief** to hand the angle to a writer.

  ### Did it move?
  A weekly chart compares your citations with the leader's on this topic. Hikoo re-scans on a schedule and tells you whether the gap narrowed, widened, or stayed flat since it flagged the topic.
</Steps>

<Tip>
The movement on this chart reflects every change since the topic was flagged, not only your action.
</Tip>

## Exports and API
