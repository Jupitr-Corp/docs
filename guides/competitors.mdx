The Competitors page shows how your citation performance stacks up against the other domains you are tracking.

## Adding competitors

You can add competitors during onboarding or at any time from the Competitors page. Enter a competitor's domain and Hikoo will begin tracking their citations alongside yours.

## Competitor ranking

A ranked table lists every tracked domain - yours and your competitors - sorted by total citation count. For each entry you can see:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Position relative to all tracked domains. |
| **Domain** | The website URL. |
| **Citations** | Total number of AI citations recorded. |
| **Share** | Percentage of all citations captured by this domain. |

## Citation share chart

A donut chart visualizes the distribution of citations across all tracked domains. This gives you an instant read on your **share of voice** in AI search results.

## Citation share over time

A historical chart tracks how each domain's citation share has changed over your selected time range. Use this to see whether you are gaining or losing ground.

## Filtering

Apply the shared **time range**, **platform**, and **localization** filters to focus on a specific market or platform.
