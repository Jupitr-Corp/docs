---

The Citations page collects every AI answer Hikoo gathered for your prompts. It shows how often you are named, linked, or used as a source, benchmarks you against competitors, and lets you open any single answer to see exactly how you were referenced.

## How your site appears

Three cards open the page, one per way your site can show up in an answer. Each shows its change against the comparison period.

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Mentions** | Answers that name your brand, or one of its alternative names. This is your visibility. |
| **Citations** | Answers that show a link to your website, inline or as a source. |
| **Background sources** | Answers where the AI read your website while searching, without showing a link to it. |

## Visibility by platform

The **Visibility by Platform** chart shows the share of each platform's answers that name your brand, over time. Below it, one card per platform gives its current value. Click a platform card to focus the chart on it, and click it again to go back to the total.

## Top sources

The **Top Sources** card lists the domains AI platforms rely on to answer your prompts. Switch between **Shown** (domains the AI linked in its answer) and **Background** (domains it read but did not show). The card also lists **Your pages** that get cited. Click **See all URLs** to open the full list.

## All answers

The **All answers** table lists every answer collected for your prompts.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Prompt** | The tracked question that produced the answer |
| **Platform** | The assistant that produced the answer |
| **Match** | How your site appears: mention, citation, or background source |
| **Position** | Where your brand is named among the brands in the answer |
| **Sentiment** | How the answer speaks about your brand. See [How sentiment works](/guides/sentiment). |
| **Competitors** | The competitors named in the same answer |
| **Sources** | The domains and pages the answer pulled from |
| **Date** | When the answer was collected |

Click a row to open the answer in a side panel. Use the arrows to move to the previous or next answer, or click **Open as a page** for the full view.

## Filters

The shared [filter bar](/guides/dashboard#the-filter-bar) sets the period, platforms, topics, markets, brand scope, intent, and tags. Above the table, you can narrow the answers further:

- **Match**: mentions, citations, or background sources.
- **Position**: top N, an exact position, or a position at N or worse.
- **Competitor**: answers that name a specific competitor.
- **Sentiment**: answers with a positive, neutral, or negative signal about your brand. Sentiment depends on your plan.

Active filters appear as chips, and **Clear all** resets them at once.

## Answer detail

The answer detail shows the full AI conversation so you can read it in context. It marks whether your site was cited, lists the competitors named in the same answer, and separates the sources into **Mentioned** (shown to the reader) and **Background** (consulted but not shown). A badge gives your position in the answer. The detail also lists the [search queries](/guides/search-queries) the assistant ran to write the answer.

## All cited URLs

**See all URLs** opens the list of every URL referenced across AI answers, sorted by frequency. You can:

- Filter by **References**: **All references**, **Shown only**, or **Background only**.
- Filter by **Origin**: **All sources**, **Your pages**, or **External only**.
- Search a URL, and sort by **Rank** or **Change**.

Each row shows the prompts that used the URL, the platforms that used it (**Used by**), and its frequency. Click a URL to see its usage over time and the answers that used it.

## Export

Click **Export** to download the answers as CSV or Excel, either all data or only the answers that match your current filters. Export depends on your plan.
