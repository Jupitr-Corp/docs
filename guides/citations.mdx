---
title: "Citations"
description: "Track every time an AI platform references your content."
---

A **citation** is recorded each time an AI platform includes a reference to your website in its response. The Citations page lets you browse, filter, and analyze these records.

## Citations chart

At the top of the page, a stacked area chart shows citation volume over time, broken down by AI platform. Use this to spot trends - for example, a spike after publishing new content or a drop that needs investigation.

## Citations table

Below the chart you will find a paginated table of every citation. Each row includes:

- The **AI platform** that produced the citation (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, etc.)
- The **date** it was recorded
- The **search query** that triggered it
- Which **competitors** were cited in the same response

Click any row to open a detail view with full context.

## Citation detail

The detail view shows:

- The complete citation context
- Platform information
- Competitors cited alongside your site
- A link to view the original AI conversation (when available)

## Filtering

Use the shared toolbar filters to narrow citations by **time range**, **platform**, and **localization**. You can also filter by a specific prompt from the [Prompts](/guides/prompts) page.
