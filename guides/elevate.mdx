**Content Suggestions** is the first page of the **Elevate** module. It lists content opportunities grounded in emerging demand, the sources AI assistants cite in your market, and what your website already covers. Each suggestion is a page you could publish to start earning AI citations.

## How suggestions appear

You do not need to start anything. Hikoo regularly researches new opportunities from your tracked prompts, topics, and pages. Each suggestion appears on the page automatically once it passes quality checks.

Every suggestion carries a short **Reason** that explains why it exists. Its **Context** panel also lists the demand signals behind it:

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Already popular query** | People already ask this a lot. |
| **Rising demand** | Interest in the question is growing. |
| **Fast rising demand** | Interest is growing quickly. |
| **Citation gap** | AI answers cite other pages on this and not yours. |
| **Uncovered query** | AI assistants search for this and your site has no page for it. |
| **Covered elsewhere** | Other publishers cover the subject and you do not. |

<Tip>
Suggestions are better when your site is fully indexed and your prompts reflect your market. Index your pages from [Indexed pages](/guides/pages) and review your [prompts](/guides/prompts).
</Tip>

## Board and table

Switch between two layouts with **Layout**:

- **Board** shows one column per status: **New**, **Accepted**, **Written**, and **Dismissed**. Drag a card to change its status, or use **Move to** on the card.
- **Table** lists suggestions with their **Format**, **Reason**, and **Added** date. Select several rows to move them to another status at once.

Narrow the list with the **Status** and **Priority** filters, and sort by **Newest first** or **Oldest first**. Priority is **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**. Use the export button in the page header to download suggestions, if your plan includes exports.

## Content types

Each suggestion recommends a format, such as an article, blog post, guide, how-to guide, tutorial, explainer, troubleshooting page, documentation page, comparison, listicle, checklist, playbook, FAQ, landing page, or research report.

## The content brief

Open a suggestion to read its brief. It reads top to bottom as the work to do:

- A summary: **Who it is for**, **What the reader gets**, the **Target query**, and the **Format**.
- An **Article outline**, with the sections to write and the questions to **Also cover**.
- **What to prove**, the claims to back up and where the proof comes from. Some proofs need input from your brand, others need further research.
- **Blocks to include**, such as a direct answer box, a comparison table, or an FAQ block, and **What you need to provide**.
- **Sources to read** before writing.
- **Internal links to place** and **Pages not to duplicate**, so the new page fits your site without competing with an existing one.
- A **Context** panel at the end with the observed demand and the reasoning behind the suggestion.

Click **Copy brief** to paste the whole brief into your editor or hand it to a writer.

## Act on a suggestion

From the brief you can:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mark as written** | Records that you published the page. |
| **Accept** | Moves the suggestion to **Accepted**, so you know you plan to write it. |
| **Dismiss** | Sets the suggestion aside. |
| **Delete** | Removes the suggestion for everyone working on this website. This cannot be undone. |

When you click **Mark as written**, enter the **Article URL**. The URL must belong to your website's domain. Hikoo indexes the page, and you can turn on **Run an audit** to analyze it right after indexing. See [Audit](/guides/audit).

## API and MCP

The REST API and the [MCP server](/guides/mcp/overview) expose content suggestions under **Content gaps**, so you can list them and read a brief from your own tools.
