Elevate analyzes your website's content against your competitors and surfaces actionable suggestions for improvement.

## How it works

1. Hikoo indexes your pages and your competitors' pages.
2. Content is compared to identify **topics your competitors cover that you do not** (or only partially cover).
3. Suggestions are generated with specific recommendations for new or improved content.

## Browsing suggestions

Each suggestion is displayed as a card showing:

- The **topic** or content gap identified
- Whether it is a **full gap** (topic completely missing) or a **partial gap** (topic insufficiently covered)
- Recommended actions to address the gap

Click a card to open a detailed view with more context, including similar competitor pages for reference.

## Managing suggestions

Every suggestion has a status you can set:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Freshly generated, not yet reviewed. |
| **Reviewed** | You have seen it but not decided. |
| **Accepted** | You plan to act on it. |
| **Dismissed** | Not relevant to your strategy. |

Use the status filter at the top of the page to focus on the suggestions that matter to you.

## Generating suggestions

Before Elevate can produce suggestions, your site needs to be indexed and content embeddings need to be generated. Navigate to the Elevate page and click **Generate** if this has not been done yet. A progress indicator shows the status.

<Note>Elevate suggestions are as good as the data they are built on. Make sure your site is fully indexed and you have added relevant competitors for the best results.</Note>
