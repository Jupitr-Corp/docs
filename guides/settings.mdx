---
title: "Settings"
description: "Manage your profile, preferences, and security settings."
---

The Settings page lets you configure your Hikoo account.

## Profile

View and update your display name. Your email address is shown for reference but cannot be changed from this page.

## Appearance

Choose between three display themes:

- **Light** - always use the light theme.
- **Dark** - always use the dark theme.
- **Auto** - follow your operating system's preference.

## Language

Hikoo is available in:

- **English**
- **Fran&ccedil;ais**

Changing the language refreshes the page and applies the new locale throughout the app.

## Security

Click **Change password** to update your account password. You will need to enter your current password and choose a new one.

<Note>If you signed up with Google, password management is handled through your Google account.</Note>

## Billing information

View and update the billing details associated with your subscription - name, company, address, and tax identifiers (VAT number or SIREN for French businesses).
