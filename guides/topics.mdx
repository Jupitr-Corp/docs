A **topic** groups your prompts into a theme. Topics keep a large prompt set organized, and they are one of the shared filter dimensions across the dashboard, so the way you organize topics shapes how you read every other view.

<Note>
In the sidebar, the topics page is labeled **Subjects**. Inside the page, in the filter bar, and on the Prompts page, the same items are called topics.
</Note>

## Manage topics

Open **Subjects** in the Spotlight section of the sidebar. From there you can:

- **Add** a topic: type its name in the first row of the list, or click **New topic**.
- **Rename** a topic from its row.
- **Delete** a topic you no longer need.
- **Search** to find a topic quickly.

Each prompt belongs to one topic. Prompts without a topic sit under **General**.

## Assign prompts to topics

- Pick a topic when you write or edit a prompt.
- On the [Prompts](/guides/prompts) page, select prompts and click **Move to topic** to reassign them in bulk.
- When you import a CSV, Hikoo can create the topics that do not exist yet.

## How topics flow through the dashboard

On the Prompts page, you can group the table by topic. **Topics** is also a filter in the shared [filter bar](/guides/dashboard#the-filter-bar), so it carries into citations, competitors, and query fanouts as you navigate. [Query fanouts](/guides/search-queries) can also be grouped by topic.

For finer slicing, add [tags](/guides/prompts#tags) to your prompts. A prompt keeps one topic but can carry several tags.

<Tip>
Keep topics aligned with how buyers think about your space, not with your internal team structure. When a topic matches a real buyer theme, filtering by it answers a real question about your visibility.
</Tip>
