A **search query**, or query fanout, is a search that an AI assistant runs internally while it answers one of your prompts. Search queries show you the keywords AI is actually searching for on your behalf, which is often not the wording you used in the prompt.

## Prompts versus search queries

The difference matters:

- A **prompt** is what you define. It is the question you want to track.
- A **search query** is what the AI actually searched to answer that prompt.

One prompt can fan out into many search queries, because the assistant rephrases and breaks your question into several searches. Reading the search queries tells you which keywords are worth targeting in your content.

## Open query fanouts

Query fanouts live inside the [Prompts](/guides/prompts) page. Open **Prompts** in the sidebar, then switch the view from **Tracked prompts** to **Query fanouts**.

## Read the list

Search queries are grouped, and each group shows its number of queries and how many times AI generated them. Use **Table settings** to group by:

- **Topic**: queries grouped by the [topic](/guides/topics) of their prompt. Queries not yet linked to a topic sit under **Unclustered**.
- **Prompt**: queries grouped by the prompt that generated them. Queries no tracked prompt is linked to anymore sit under **Without a prompt**.

Each query row shows the **Platforms** that ran it, its number of **Generations**, and when it was **Last seen**.

You can also:

- **Search** for a query.
- **Sort by** **Most generated**, **Most queries**, or **Recently detected**.
- **Expand all** or **Collapse all** groups at once.
- **Copy** a query to reuse it elsewhere.
- Narrow the list with the shared [filter bar](/guides/dashboard#the-filter-bar): period, platforms, topics, markets, brand scope, intent, and tags.
- **Export** the list from the more actions menu.

## Query detail

Click a search query to see:

- **Generations over time**, showing when it was issued.
- The **Platforms** that issued it.
- The **Source prompts** it came from.

## Search queries behind one answer

Each answer in [Citations](/guides/citations) also has an **AI Search Queries** section that lists the searches the assistant ran to write it, which keeps you focused on the answer you are investigating.
