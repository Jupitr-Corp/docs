## Available plans

Hikoo offers several subscription tiers:

| Plan | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Lite** | Getting started with basic citation monitoring. |
| **Growth** | Teams that need more prompts, audits, and website capacity. |
| **Scale** | High-volume monitoring across many websites and markets. |
| **Custom** | Enterprise needs - contact us for a tailored package. |

Each plan specifies limits for websites, prompts, indexed pages, monthly audits, and health-check refreshes. You can compare plans side by side on the Billing page.

## Upgrading or changing plans

1. Go to **Billing** from the sidebar.
2. Browse the available plans.
3. Click **Upgrade** on the plan you want.
4. Complete the checkout flow.

Changes take effect immediately. Quotas reset at the start of each billing period.

## Checkout

During checkout you will be asked to provide:

- **Account type** - Individual, Micro-entrepreneur, Company, or EU/Non-EU entity.
- **Billing information** - name, company name, address.
- **Tax identifiers** - EU VAT number or French SIREN, if applicable (validated automatically).
- **Payment method** - processed securely through Stripe.

## Managing your subscription

Click **Manage subscription** on the Billing page to open the customer portal where you can:

- Update your payment method
- View invoices
- Change or cancel your plan
