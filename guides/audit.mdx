---
title: "Audit"
description: "Run website audits to evaluate page quality and track your score over time."
---

The Audit feature crawls your website and evaluates each page against a set of quality criteria, giving you an overall score and page-level details.

## Running an audit

You have three options:

- **Full website audit** - crawl and evaluate your entire site.
- **Single page audit** - audit a specific URL.
- **Selected pages audit** - choose a set of pages to evaluate.

Click **New audit** and select the scope. The audit runs in the background - a banner shows progress while it is in process.

## Audit results

Once complete, an audit shows:

- **Overall score** - a percentage displayed as a ring chart.
- **Pages analyzed** - the total number of pages evaluated.
- **Average page score** - the mean score across all pages.
- **Assessment** - a summary rating (Excellent, Good, Needs work, or Poor).

## Page-level details

Click into an audit to see individual page results. Each page lists:

- Its URL and category (article, product, service, etc.)
- An individual quality score
- Any errors or warnings detected
- Specific recommendations for improvement

## Audit quotas

Your subscription plan includes a monthly audit allowance. A counter on the Audit page shows how many audits you have remaining in the current billing period.

## Indexed pages

After an audit, all discovered pages are available on the **Pages** tab. You can filter by:

- **Status** - OK, Error, or Warning
- **Category** - Article, Product, Service, Other
- **Language**

Use this view to quickly find pages that need attention.
