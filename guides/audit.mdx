**Audit** is the first page of the **Analyzer** module. An audit scores how readable your pages are for AI engines, across structure, content, and technical signals, and lists what to fix first. Scores roll up into an overall score you can track over time.

The **Analyzer** module has three pages:

- **Audit**, page-by-page scoring, covered here.
- **Health & Suggestions**, a site-wide readiness check. See [Health](/guides/health).
- **Indexed Pages**, the inventory of pages Hikoo knows. See [Indexed pages](/guides/pages).

## Launch an audit

Click **Launch audit** to open the dialog. It has two tabs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Full Site">
    Audit every indexed page of the website. The dialog shows how many pages were detected.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Select Pages">
    Pick pages from your indexed pages. To audit a page that is not indexed, paste its full URL and add it as a custom URL. Use this tab to check a single page after you change it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The dialog shows how many pages you can still audit this period, and warns you when a selection is larger than what remains. Click **Launch Audit** to start.

The audit runs in the background. A **Running Audits** banner shows its progress. You can leave the page and come back.

<Note>
Audits are scoped to the website selected in the site switcher. Switch sites in the sidebar to audit a different website.
</Note>

## The Audit page

Once you have completed audits, the page shows:

- Your **Average score** out of 100, compared with the previous audit.
- **Score history**, one bar per audit. Filter it by **Scope** to compare full-site and selected-page audits separately. Click a bar to open that report.
- **Completed audits**, the number of audits finished so far.
- **Pages audited this month**, your usage against your plan limit and when it resets.

Below, the **Audit History** table lists every audit with its **Date**, **Scope**, **Pages**, **Score**, change **vs previous**, and **Status**. Filter it by scope or score range. Click **View report** to open an audit, or use **Re-run this audit** to launch a new audit on the same pages. Use the export button in the page header to download your audits, if your plan includes exports.

## How pages are scored

Each page is scored against a weighted catalog of criteria. Static checks read your page directly. LLM checks use a model to judge qualities that are hard to measure mechanically, such as whether headings read like real questions.

Criteria depend on the page type, so a blog post is judged by different rules than a product page. A set of site-wide checks also applies to the website as a whole.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Site-wide checks" icon="globe">
    - `llms.txt` exists and is well formed.
    - `robots.txt` exists and allows AI crawlers.
    - `sitemap.xml` exists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="On-page checks" icon="file-text">
    - Relevant structured data, such as an Article schema, is present as JSON-LD.
    - The page has a clean heading hierarchy.
    - The page loads fast on mobile.
    - No content is hidden behind JavaScript.
    - Headings are descriptive and question-style.
    - Metadata is strong.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Audit report

Open an audit to see its report:

- The **Overall score** out of 100.
- **Pages audited** and **Average page score**.
- **Pages by score**, the distribution of pages across score bands: 80 and above, 60 to 79, and below 60.
- **Scores by page type**, the average score of each page type, weakest first.
- The list of audited pages, which you can filter.

Click **Sync** on the report to launch a new audit with the same pages.

## Page detail

Click a page in the report to open its detail. The **Audit Criteria** tab lists every criterion with its result, the points it is worth, and **How to fix** it when it fails. The header shows the page score, its pass rate, and what is left **To fix**.

## Quotas

Your plan determines how many pages you can audit each month. The **Pages audited this month** card and the launch dialog show what you have left and when the count resets.

## API and MCP

You can list audits, launch them, and read page results from the REST API and the [MCP server](/guides/mcp/overview).
