**Hiko** is the assistant built into Hikoo. It reads your citations, prompts, competitors, sources, and audits, and answers your questions in plain language. Every answer links back to the exact page your data lives on, so you can check it.

## Open Hiko

You can reach Hiko two ways:

- **Full view.** Use the **Dashboard** / **Hiko** switch at the top of the sidebar.
- **Side panel.** Click **Ask Hiko** in the header of any dashboard page. Hiko reads the page you are on along with your question, and each question asked from a page starts a fresh conversation. Click **Open full view** to continue in the full view.

## Ask a question

Type your question in the composer and press Enter. Shift+Enter adds a new line. To get started, pick one of the suggested questions, or click **Capabilities** to see what Hiko can analyze:

| Area | Example question |
|---|---|
| **Citations** | Where and how often am I cited right now? |
| **Competitors** | Which competitors are ahead of me on my topics? |
| **Sources** | Which sources should I target first? |
| **Search queries** | On which queries do I appear? |
| **Prompts** | Which prompts have the lowest visibility? |
| **Technical audit** | What is my latest audit score? |
| **Topics** | Which topics am I currently tracking? |

The empty state also shows **What moved this week**: your visibility change, the gap with your closest competitor, and new competitors spotted, each with a follow-up question you can ask in one click.

Use the **Period Hiko reads** selector to choose the data Hiko looks at: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 12 months**, **All time**, or a **Custom period**.

## Read an answer

- **Sources** under each answer open the Hikoo pages the data comes from.
- **View activity** shows the steps Hiko took, such as checking citations or analyzing competitors, and what each step found.
- **Copy** puts the answer on your clipboard.

Hiko is an AI and can make mistakes. Check important information on the linked pages.

## Let Hiko make changes

Hiko can prepare changes to your setup, and you stay in control. When you ask for a change, Hiko shows a proposal card with the exact edit. Nothing happens until you click **Confirm**. Click **Cancel** to drop it.

Hiko can propose to:

- Create, edit, archive, or restore [prompts](/guides/prompts).
- Create, rename, or delete [topics](/guides/topics), and move prompts to a topic.
- Add, edit, or remove [competitors](/guides/competitors), and add or dismiss suggested competitors.

Hiko can also apply filters to the page you are on, for example to focus on one platform or one topic.

## Conversation history

Open **Conversation history** to see your past conversations, grouped by date. You can search them, rename them, or delete them. Click **New conversation** to start over.

## Limits and availability

Your plan determines whether Hiko is included and how many messages you can send each day. Hiko shows how many messages you have left today, and the limit resets the next day.
