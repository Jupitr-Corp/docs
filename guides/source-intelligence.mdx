---
title: "Source intelligence"
description: "See every page AI assistants cite in your market, who is listed on it, and where you should appear"
---

**Source Intelligence** lists the pages that AI assistants cite when they answer your tracked prompts. For each page, Hikoo checks which brands it lists, you included, and ranks the pages by how much you would gain by appearing there. It answers one practical question: where do I need to be listed or mentioned to earn AI citations?

The page is part of the **Battlemap** module. It updates as new AI citations are collected. Trends compare the last 30 days with the 30 days before.

<Info>
Source Intelligence is available on plans that include it. On other plans, the page shows a blurred preview with an invitation to upgrade.
</Info>

The page has two views. Switch between them with the **View** control at the top:

- **Sources**, every cited web page, covered below.
- **Social networks**, the content AI assistants cite on YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook. See [Social networks](#social-networks).

## Status tabs

Tabs at the top of **Sources** split pages by your relation to them, with a count on each:

| Tab | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every source. |
| **You appear** | Pages that list you, alone or with competitors. |
| **Lost ground** | Competitors are listed and you are not. |
| **Contested** | You and competitors are both listed. |
| **Open** | AI assistants cite the page, but no tracked brand is listed on it yet. |
| **Only you** | You are the only tracked brand on the page. |

Turn on **Avoid competitor pages** to set aside pages hosted on your competitors' own sites. Other sources take their place.

## Filters, sort, and views

Narrow the list with the toolbar:

- **Search** by page or domain.
- **Type** to focus on one source family: affiliate, comparison, review, media, community, video, or reference.
- **Trend** to show only sources that are new, gaining, slowing down, or no longer cited.
- A platform filter to focus on one or more AI assistants.
- **Sort** by **Opportunity**, **Moving**, **Most cited**, or **Quick wins**.

Use **View** to switch between **By article**, one row per cited page, and **By domain**, pages grouped under their publisher.

## The sources table

Each row is one cited page.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Source** | The page, its type, and marks such as **Affiliate**, **Sponsored**, or how long ago the page was updated. |
| **Why** | Why the page matters, for example how many rivals are listed and not you, or how many AI answers cite it without you. |
| **Score** | The opportunity score out of 100. |
| **12 weeks** | Citations per week over the last 12 weeks. |
| **Action** | What to do next, or your status on the page, such as **Cited** or **On page**. |

Above the table, **Winning this selection** estimates the visibility you would gain if you were cited on every filtered source.

Pages Hikoo has not read yet appear in a separate group. Their type may be guessed from the URL until the analysis runs. Low-quality sources, such as tracking URLs or pages you cannot be listed on, are hidden automatically.

## Source dossier

Click a row to open its dossier in a side panel. It reads top to bottom: the decision, the move, then the evidence.

- An **opportunity** score out of 100, with **Why** it scores that way. The score weighs the competitors present, whether you are absent, citation volume, and how many AI engines cite the page.
- A verdict on who is listed on the page and an estimate of the visibility gain if you win it.
- A **Recommended play** with concrete steps and an effort level, for example finding the editor and pitching an inclusion, claiming a profile, or replying in a thread.
- **Citation history**, with weekly citations and the first and last time the page was cited.
- Who the page cites, with the brands actually listed on the page kept apart from brands AI answers only associate with it.
- **Where it's cited**, the AI answers that used the page.

At the bottom, the dossier offers the next action:

- Open the page to act on it. The button matches the page type, for example **Get listed**, **Get reviewed**, **Submit**, or **Reply**.
- Click **I'm on it** after you act. Hikoo checks the page and tells you whether your brand is now **On the page** or not yet. Use **Re-check** later to check again.
- If Hikoo cannot open the page, for example because of a login wall or bot protection, confirm it yourself with **Yes, I'm on it**.
- Click **Don't appear here** to hide a source you do not want to pursue. Hidden sources move to the **Hidden** section below the table, where you can **Restore** them.

<Note>
Competitor-owned pages are marked **Not pursuable**, because they are not a realistic outreach target. Your own pages carry a reminder to optimize them directly.
</Note>

## Social networks

The **Social networks** view shows which social content AI answers cite when they talk about your market. A tile per network gives the number of AI answers that cite it and whether you appear in them.

For each network you can rank channels or accounts, contents, and communities, and switch to **Where you are absent**. A **Recommendations** list ranks the best moves, such as replying in threads, offering a collaboration, or contacting an account. Open any entry to see the answers that cite it, the brands named in them, and a suggested next step.

## Pitch sources with Outreach

To have Hikoo contact source owners for you, use [Outreach](/guides/outreach/overview). It works from the same sources and tracks every pitch and reply.

## Exports and API

Use the export button in the page header to download the list, with your current type and engine filters, if your plan includes exports.

The REST API and the [MCP server](/guides/mcp/overview) expose the same data, including a per-URL list of source pages with your relation to each page, who is listed on it, and its trend. See [List source pages](/api-reference/source-intelligence/list-source-pages).
