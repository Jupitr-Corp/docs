**Shopping** shows which products of your Shopify store appear in the AI answers collected for your prompts. Use it to spot the products AI assistants recommend, and the ones they never mention.

## Before you start

Shopping reads your catalogue from Shopify, so the site must be connected to its store. Connect it from the **Shopify** integration, in the **Integrations** tab of your workspace settings. Once the store is connected, **Shopping** appears in the Spotlight section of the sidebar.

If the connection breaks, for example when the app is uninstalled or is missing an access scope, the page tells you what to fix and links to the Shopify integration.

## When a product appears

A product appears in an answer when its page is among the answer's sources, or when the answer names it. Products with a one-word name only count through their page, to avoid false matches.

## What the page shows

A summary at the top tells you how many of your products appear in AI answers, and how many answers Hikoo analyzed.

Below it, one card per product shows:

- The number of answers it appears in.
- How many times its page was used as a source, and how many times it was named.
- When it was last seen in an answer, or **Never seen**.

## Filters

- The shared [filter bar](/guides/dashboard#the-filter-bar) sets the period, platforms, topics, markets, brand scope, intent, and tags.
- **Presence** narrows the grid to **All products**, **Seen**, or **Never seen**.

<Tip>
If no product appears yet, your tracked prompts may be too broad. Add buying prompts, such as "best organic moisturizer", on the [Prompts](/guides/prompts) page.
</Tip>
