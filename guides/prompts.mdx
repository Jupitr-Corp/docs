A **prompt** is a natural-language question that Hikoo runs against AI assistants to see whether your brand gets named. Your prompts are the tracked query set behind every answer, so writing good ones is the foundation of the whole dashboard.

The Prompts page has two views: **Tracked prompts** (described here) and **Query fanouts**, the searches AI assistants run to answer your prompts. See [Query fanouts](/guides/search-queries).

## Status tabs

Tabs above the table sort your prompts by health: **All**, **Winning**, **At risk**, **Unclaimed**, and **Archived**. Each prompt carries a status based on its visibility score and its 7-day trend:

| Status | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Winning** | Score of 70 or more, with a stable trend. |
| **At risk** | Score between 30 and 69, or a trend of -10 or worse. |
| **Losing** | Score under 30, with a negative trend. |
| **Unclaimed** | Your brand was never named. |

## The prompts table

Each row summarizes how you are doing on one question:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Prompt** | The question sent to every tracked platform, with its tags underneath. |
| **Visibility** | The share of this prompt's answers that name your brand, with its change. |
| **Intent** | The search intent: informational, comparative, transactional, or navigational. |
| **Volume** | The estimated monthly AI search demand behind the prompt. Depends on your plan. |
| **Named by** | The AI platforms whose answers name your brand. |
| **Markets** | The countries or regions the prompt is tracked in. |
| **Sentiment** | The overall tone of AI mentions about your brand. See [How sentiment works](/guides/sentiment). |
| **Stage** | The buyer journey stage: discovery, consideration, decision, or retention. |
| **Scope** | **Branded** if the prompt names your brand, **Non-branded** otherwise. |
| **Status** | Winning, at risk, losing, or unclaimed. |

The shared [filter bar](/guides/dashboard#the-filter-bar) applies here too, so you can focus on a period, platforms, topics, markets, brand scope, intent, or tags. Use **Search** to find a prompt by its text.

In **Table settings**, group the table by **Topic**, **Intent**, **Journey stage**, or **None**.

## Add prompts

Click **Add** to open the add menu.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Write a prompt">
    Write a single prompt yourself, then pick its topic, markets, and classification. Use this when you know the exact question you want to track.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Generate with AI">
    Let Hikoo analyze your site and draft prompts, grouped by customer journey stage. Add optional keywords to steer it. Review the suggestions, **Regenerate** for more, and add the ones that fit.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Import CSV">
    Upload a CSV with a `content` column, plus optional `topic`, `intent`, `journey_stage`, `brand_scope`, and `localizations` columns. Download the **Sample file** for the format. Hikoo validates every row and shows duplicates, errors, and rows over quota before you click **Import**. It can also create topics that do not exist yet.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Each country a prompt is tracked in counts as one prompt of your plan. A prompt tracked in 3 countries uses 3 prompts.
</Note>

## Classify prompts

Classification helps you slice your prompts by how buyers actually ask. Each prompt can carry:

- A **search intent**: informational, comparative, transactional, or navigational.
- A **journey stage**: discovery, consideration, decision, or retention.
- A **brand scope**: branded or non-branded. Hikoo classifies it automatically, and you can override it.
- A **source keyword** it came from.

Hikoo fills these values automatically, so you can classify a large set without tagging each one by hand.

## Tags

Tags are free labels on your prompts, alongside topics. A prompt keeps one [topic](/guides/topics) but can carry several tags. Add tags from the prompt editor, or open **Tags** from the more actions menu to rename, recolor, merge, or delete them. Tags then appear as a filter in the shared filter bar.

## Bulk actions

Select prompts to act on them at once: **Classify** (intent, journey stage, brand scope), **Tags**, **Retarget market**, **Move to topic**, **Archive**, **Export**, or **Delete**.

## Archive and restore

Archive a prompt from its row menu, or in bulk, to stop tracking it without losing it.

- An archived prompt is no longer tracked and does not count toward your quota, so archiving frees a slot.
- Its history stays in your analytics.
- Open the **Archived** tab and click **Restore** to track it again. If your plan has no free slot, Hikoo asks you to pick an active prompt to archive in exchange. **Swap** does the same on purpose.

Deleting a prompt cannot be undone. Existing answers stay attached to your history.

## Prompts beyond your plan

Your plan sets how many prompts each website can track, and the usage counter at the top of the page shows your current use. When you have more prompts than your plan allows, for example after a downgrade, the extra prompts are archived and marked **Beyond your plan**. The **Archived** tab shows how many are beyond your plan.

<Warning>
Prompts beyond your plan are permanently deleted once the countdown on their row reaches zero. Restore them in exchange for other prompts, or click **Upgrade for more slots**.
</Warning>

You can also add prompt packs from billing to track more prompts without changing plan.

## Prompt detail

Click a prompt to open its detail panel:

- **Visibility (last 30 days)**, with its trend
- Your **Best rank** and **Coverage** (how many platforms name you)
- A **Per-platform breakdown**
- **Prompt volume** over time, on plans that include it
- The **Answers** AI assistants gave, newest first, with their sentiment
- The competitors named alongside you

Use the arrows to move to the previous or next prompt.

<Tip>
Write prompts the way real users ask AI assistants. Natural, specific questions match how people actually query, which makes your tracking reflect real demand.
</Tip>
