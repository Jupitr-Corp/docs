A **prompt** is a search query you want Hikoo to monitor across AI platforms. When any platform cites your site (or a competitor) in response to that query, Hikoo records the citation.

## Creating a prompt

1. Go to the **Prompts** page.
2. Click **Create prompt**.
3. Enter the query text - this should be a question or instruction a real user might type into an AI assistant.
4. Optionally assign it to a **topic** to keep prompts organized.

<Tip>Write prompts that reflect how your audience actually searches. Natural, conversational queries produce more realistic tracking results.</Tip>

## Prompt list

Prompts are displayed grouped by topic. For each prompt you can see:

- The query text
- A **visibility percentage** - how often your site is cited relative to competitors for this prompt
- The number of citations it has generated

Click a prompt to view its detailed performance breakdown, including citation trends over time, platform distribution, and competitor analysis.

## Prompt limits

Your subscription plan determines how many prompts you can create per website. A progress bar shows your current usage. If you reach your limit, you can upgrade your plan or delete prompts you no longer need.

## Bulk operations

Select multiple prompts and use **Batch delete** to remove them in one action.
