Open **Settings** from the user menu, then **Security**. The section has four parts: your password, two-factor authentication, your data, and a danger zone.

## Change your password

Click **Change Password**, enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password** twice. A password needs at least 10 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number. Hikoo refuses a password that appears in a known data breach.

<Note>If you signed up with Google, your account has no Hikoo password. You sign in through Google instead.</Note>

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in. You can turn on several methods, and choose any of them at sign-in.

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Authenticator app** | Marked **Recommended**. Use an app like Google Authenticator or 1Password to generate a 6-digit code. |
| **Email** | Marked **Backup**. Hikoo emails you a one-time code at sign-in. |
| **Passkeys** | Sign in with Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a security key. |
| **Recovery codes** | One-time codes for when you lose access to your other methods. |

### Set up an authenticator app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the setup">
    Next to **Authenticator app**, click **Set up**, then **Set up authenticator app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Hikoo to your app">
    Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you cannot scan it, enter the **Setup key** shown below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a code">
    Enter the code your app shows in **Code from the app**, then click **Enable**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set up email codes

Next to **Email**, click **Set up**, then **Send a code**. Enter the code Hikoo emails you and click **Enable**.

### Add a passkey

In **Passkeys**, click **Add passkey** and follow your browser or device prompt. Each passkey shows when it was added and last used. You can **Rename** or **Remove** it later.

<Tip>If the passkey is not created and you use a password manager, check that the password manager is unlocked, or use your device's built-in security instead.</Tip>

### Save your recovery codes

When you turn on your first method, Hikoo shows your recovery codes once. **Copy**, **Download**, or **Print** them, store them somewhere safe, then confirm **I have saved my recovery codes**. Each code works once.

The **Recovery codes** row shows how many codes you have left. Click **Regenerate codes** to get a new set, which replaces the old one.

### Sign in with two-factor authentication

After your password, Hikoo asks for your second step. It offers the method you used last. Click **Use a different method** to switch to a passkey, your authenticator app, an email code, or a recovery code.

### Turn off a method

Click **Disable** next to a method and confirm with your password or another of your methods. That method is no longer asked for at sign-in.

<Info>A workspace owner or admin can turn on **Require two-factor authentication** in the workspace **Settings** section. Members without two-factor authentication must then add a method before they can use that workspace.</Info>

## Export your data

Under **Your data**, **Export your data** downloads a copy of your personal data: your account, preferences, billing, and sites. Click **Download JSON** or **Download CSV**.

## Delete your account

Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete account**. Type your email address to confirm, enter your password, then click **Delete my account**.

<Warning>Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Your solo workspaces and their data are removed.</Warning>
