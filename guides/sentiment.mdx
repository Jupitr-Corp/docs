**Sentiment** tells you how AI assistants speak about your brand in their answers. It has no page of its own: you find it as a column and a filter on the pages where you read answers.

## How Hikoo reads an answer

Hikoo highlights every opinionated phrase about your brand in an answer and tags it:

- **Positive**: praise, a recommendation, or satisfaction.
- **Neutral**: a factual reference without an opinion.
- **Negative**: criticism, a complaint, or dissatisfaction.

From those highlights, each answer gets an overall reading: **Positive**, **Neutral**, **Negative**, or **Mixed** when it holds both praise and criticism.

## The sentiment score

To summarize many answers, Hikoo computes a score from 0 to 100:

`score = 50 + 50 × (positive - negative) / total`

A score of 100 means every highlight is positive, 0 means every highlight is negative, and 50 is balanced. On the Prompts page, a score of 70 or more reads as positive and a score of 30 or less reads as negative. A prompt needs a few highlights before it gets a score.

## Where you see sentiment

| Where | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Citations](/guides/citations) | A **Sentiment** column in the answers table, and a **Sentiment** filter to keep only answers with a positive, neutral, or negative signal. |
| [Prompts](/guides/prompts) | A **Sentiment** column with the score of each prompt, and the sentiment of each answer in the prompt detail, with an explanation of how it was read. |
| [MCP server](/guides/mcp/overview) and API | A sentiment summary and a sentiment trend over time. |

Sentiment depends on your plan. On plans without it, the **Sentiment** filter is hidden and the answer detail shows an upgrade prompt instead.

<Tip>
Filter citations on **Negative** to read the exact phrases behind a drop, then fix the source or the page AI relies on.
</Tip>
