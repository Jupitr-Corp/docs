**Agent Analytics** shows the real AI agent and crawler traffic hitting your own site. It tells you which AI providers visit you, why they come, which URLs they read, and how that activity changes over time.

## How this differs from citations

Citations and agent analytics answer two different questions:

- **Citations** measure whether AI answers name or link you. See [Citations](/guides/citations).
- **Agent analytics** measures whether AI bots actually fetch your pages.

A page can be crawled without being cited, and cited without being freshly crawled, so it helps to watch both.

## What the page shows

### Visits over time

The **Visits over time** chart plots every AI agent visit for the period. Above it, **AI agent visits** and **Pages visited** give the totals, with their change against the comparison period.

### Providers

The **Providers** ranking lists the AI companies whose agents visit your site, ranked by visits. Each row shows the provider's **Share of visits**, its **Trend**, and its number of **Visits**. Click **Details** on a provider to switch the chart to its individual agents, and **Back to total** to return.

### Pages crawled by AI agents

The **Pages crawled by AI agents** table lists the URLs agents read, with the number of **Bot visits** and the **Agents** that made them. Use the search field to find a URL.

## Filters

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Period** | A preset or a custom range, plus a comparison period for the changes. |
| **Providers** | One or more AI providers, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, or Google. Starts on **All providers**. |
| **Intent** | Why the agent fetched the page. Starts on **All intents**. |

The intents are:

| Intent | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Answering a user** | An assistant fetched the page live to answer someone's question. |
| **AI search index** | A crawler built the search index an assistant uses. |
| **Autonomous agent** | An agent browsed the page while carrying out a task. |
| **Training** | A crawler collected content to train a model. |
| **Other AI bots** | AI bots that fit none of the above. |

## Connect your site

Agent analytics needs your site's request logs. Hikoo keeps only the visits of tracked AI agents (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and about a hundred more) and ignores human visits and other bots. Each site has its own connection key, which Hikoo expects in an `X-Agent-Token` header.

Click **Configuration** on the Agent Analytics page, or **Configure the connection** on the empty page, to open the setup. The connection also lives in the **Integrations** tab of your workspace settings.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sites hosted on Vercel">
    Vercel sends your site's logs to Hikoo through a Log Drain. Log Drains are available to Vercel teams on the Pro or Enterprise plan.

    <Steps>
      ### Create the connection key

      In Hikoo, open the **Vercel** integration for your site and click **Create the key**.

      ### Add a drain in Vercel

      In Vercel, open the team that hosts the site, go to **Team Settings**, then **Drains**, and click **Add Drain**. Choose **Logs** as the data to send.

      ### Pick the project and sources

      Choose **Specific Projects** and select the site's project. Tick **Functions**, **Edge Functions**, **Static Files**, **Rewrites**, and **Redirects**. Keep the **Production** environment only and leave the sampling rate at 100%.

      ### Set the destination

      Choose **Custom Endpoint**. Paste the endpoint URL shown in Hikoo, pick JSON or NDJSON, and add a custom header named `X-Agent-Token` with your key as its value.

      ### Save and check

      Save the drain and come back to the integration. The site switches to **Connected** as soon as Vercel sends its first logs, usually a few seconds after someone visits the site.
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Other hosts">
    Hikoo also accepts AI agent visits from a proxy or middleware running in front of your site, on any host. For each request, your proxy sends the visited URL and the user agent to Hikoo, along with your site's key in the `X-Agent-Token` header. Hikoo classifies the visitor and stores only AI agents.

    The in-app guide covers Vercel only. To set up another host, write to [support@tryhikoo.com](mailto:support@tryhikoo.com) and the team gives you the endpoint and the request format.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Visits show up in Agent Analytics, the public API, and the MCP server shortly after the bot's visit.

<Warning>
Treat your connection key like a password. Anyone who has it can send visits for your site.
</Warning>

### Disconnect a site

Click **Disconnect** next to the site in the integration. Hikoo then refuses new logs for that site, and the visits already stored stay in your analytics. On Vercel, also delete the drain, otherwise Vercel keeps sending it and flags it as failing. If a site is disconnected, a banner on the Agent Analytics page offers to **Reconnect** it.

## Availability

Your plan determines whether agent analytics is included. On a plan without it, the page is hidden and incoming logs are ignored.
