The Health page checks whether your website follows best practices for being discoverable and citable by AI platforms.

## Health signals

Hikoo evaluates the following signals:

| Signal | What it checks |
|---|---|
| **robots.txt AI access** | Whether your `robots.txt` file allows AI crawlers to access your content. |
| **llms.txt** | Whether your site publishes an `llms.txt` file - a standard for signaling AI-ready content. |
| **Sitemap** | Whether a valid sitemap is present to help crawlers discover your pages. |

Each signal is shown as a status card - passing, failing, or not yet checked.

## Improvement suggestions

Below the status cards, Hikoo provides AI-generated suggestions to improve your site's health. These are tailored recommendations based on your current configuration.

Click **Regenerate suggestions** to refresh them after making changes to your site.

## Refreshing health checks

Health data is collected automatically when you add a website. To re-check at any time, click **Refresh**. Health refreshes count against your monthly quota (visible on the page).

## llms.txt generator

If your site does not have an `llms.txt` file yet, Hikoo includes a built-in generator tool. Provide your site URL and Hikoo will produce a ready-to-use `llms.txt` file you can add to your domain root.
