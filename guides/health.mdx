**Health & Suggestions** is part of the **Analyzer** module. It answers one question: is your website technically ready to be discovered and cited by AI? It also turns what Hikoo learned from your pages into prioritized suggestions.

Health is distinct from the per-page [audit](/guides/audit). The audit scores individual pages. Health looks at the website as a whole: the files and crawler rules that decide whether AI assistants can reach your content at all.

## Overview

The top of the page sums up four things:

- **Health checks**, how many site-wide checks pass.
- **AI crawlers allowed**, and how many are blocked.
- **Open suggestions**, split between pages to improve and pages to create.
- **Indexed pages**, with the date of the last indexation.

## Health checks

Hikoo runs three site-wide checks on your domain.

| Check | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| **AI Crawlers** | Whether your `robots.txt` allows AI crawlers. |
| **llms.txt** | Whether your site publishes an `llms.txt` file. |
| **Sitemap** | Whether a sitemap is present so crawlers can discover your pages. |

## GEO resources

The **GEO resources** card shows the sitemap, `llms.txt`, and `robots.txt` URLs Hikoo uses for these checks, and whether each one was detected. Click **Configure URLs** to set a custom URL for a resource, for example a sitemap that does not live at the default location. Leave a field empty to probe the default location. Detected URLs fill in automatically on each refresh.

## AI crawlers

The **AI crawlers** card shows which AI crawlers your `robots.txt` allows or blocks. Each crawler carries an importance (**Critical**, **High**, or **Medium**) and a purpose:

- **Search** crawlers index your site so the AI can cite it in answers.
- **User** crawlers fetch your page live when someone asks the AI about it.
- **Training** crawlers read your content to train the model.

Tracked crawlers include:

| Crawler | Owner |
|---|---|
| GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User | OpenAI |
| ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User | Anthropic |
| Googlebot, Google-Extended, Gemini-Deep-Research | Google |
| PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User | Perplexity |
| Bingbot | Microsoft (Bing) |
| MistralAI-User | Mistral |
| Bytespider | ByteDance |
| Amazonbot | Amazon |
| meta-externalagent, meta-externalfetcher, FacebookBot | Meta |
| DuckAssistBot | DuckDuckGo |

If your `robots.txt` still uses directives no crawler reads anymore, Hikoo flags them and tells you what to use instead.

<Warning>
Blocking a search or user crawler can stop an AI assistant from reaching your content when it answers a user. Check the importance before you block a crawler.
</Warning>

### Bot protection

If a firewall answers Hikoo's robot differently from a regular browser, a banner explains that bot protection is filtering robots on your site. This does not prove AI crawlers are blocked, since many firewalls let known AI crawlers through after checking their IP address. When [Agent analytics](/guides/agent-analytics) is available, the banner also says whether AI crawler visits were recorded over the last 30 days.

If the crawler cannot read some of your pages at all, contact [support@tryhikoo.com](mailto:support@tryhikoo.com) to move forward.

## Suggestions

Suggestions appear once your pages are indexed. They come in two groups:

- **Improvements**, fixes on existing pages, such as a missing schema, author, publication date, FAQ section, meta description, or H1.
- **Content to Create**, pages your site is missing, such as an FAQ page, a glossary, a comparison page, or case studies.

Each suggestion has a priority and the number of affected pages. For each one you can:

- Click **View** to see the affected pages.
- **Dismiss** a suggestion you do not want. **Restore** it later from the dismissed list.

Click **Regenerate suggestions** to rebuild the full set after you change your site.

<Info>
Indexation now lives on [Indexed pages](/guides/pages). If no page is indexed yet, Health links you there to start one.
</Info>

## Refresh the checks

Health data is collected when you add a website. After that, Hikoo refreshes it automatically. Click **Refresh checks** to run the checks on demand.

<Note>
Refreshes and suggestion regenerations count against monthly quotas set by your plan.
</Note>

## API and MCP

You can read health data, refresh it, and dismiss, restore, or regenerate suggestions from the REST API and the [MCP server](/guides/mcp/overview).
