Hikoo answers one question: when people ask AI assistants about your space, does your brand show up, and what can you do about it. This page explains the ideas that everything else builds on.

## From prompts to answers

<Steps>

### You define prompts

A **prompt** is a natural-language question a real user might ask an AI assistant, for example "best project management tool for startups". You write prompts, import them, or let Hikoo generate them. See [Prompts](/guides/prompts).

### Hikoo asks the AI assistants

Hikoo sends your prompts to the AI assistants and records their full answers. Behind each answer the assistant runs its own **search queries** (query fanouts), which Hikoo also captures. See [Query fanouts](/guides/search-queries).

### Hikoo reads every answer

For each answer, Hikoo checks how your site appears, which competitors are named, in what position, which sources were used, and what tone the answer takes about you. See [Citations](/guides/citations).

</Steps>

## How your site can appear in an answer

| Match | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Mention** | The answer names your brand, or one of its alternative names. Readers see your name. |
| **Citation** | The answer shows a link to your website, inline or as a source. Readers can click through. |
| **Background source** | The assistant read your website while searching, but shows no link to it. |

## The metrics you will see

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Visibility** | The share of AI answers that name your brand, out of every answer collected in the period. |
| **Share of voice** | Your mentions as a share of the mentions of every tracked brand. Each answer counts once per brand. |
| **Citations** | The share of answers that show a link to your website. |
| **Position** | Where your brand is named in an answer (1 means named first). |
| **Sentiment** | Whether an answer speaks positively, neutrally, or negatively about your brand. See [How sentiment works](/guides/sentiment). |

## How the app is organized

The sidebar groups features into modules. This documentation follows the same structure.

| Module | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The [dashboard](/guides/dashboard). The **Dashboard** / **Hiko** switch at the top of the sidebar opens [Hiko](/guides/hiko), the assistant that answers questions about your data. |
| **Spotlight** | [Citations](/guides/citations), [prompts](/guides/prompts) and their [query fanouts](/guides/search-queries), [subjects](/guides/topics), [agent analytics](/guides/agent-analytics), and [shopping](/guides/shopping). |
| **Battlemap** | [Competitors](/guides/competitors), [source intelligence](/guides/source-intelligence), and [outreach](/guides/outreach/overview). |
| **Elevate** | [Content suggestions](/guides/elevate) where competitors get cited and you do not. |
| **Analyzer** | [Audit](/guides/audit), [health](/guides/health), and [pages](/guides/pages). |

Some items only appear when they apply to you. For example, **Shopping** appears once the site is connected to Shopify, and **Agent Analytics** depends on your plan.

## Spotlight: where your visibility lives

Spotlight is where you read what AI assistants say. Start with [Citations](/guides/citations) to browse the answers, then use [Prompts](/guides/prompts) to see which questions you win, lose, or have not claimed yet.

## Everything is scoped to a website

Every metric belongs to a specific **website**. Use the site switcher in the sidebar to change the active site, and the shared filter bar (period, platforms, topics, markets, brand scope, intent, tags) to slice any view. Websites live inside [workspaces](/guides/workspaces), which hold your team and your subscription.
