Hikoo is a GEO and AEO platform (Generative and Answer Engine Optimization). It shows you how AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews talk about your brand when they answer real questions. It benchmarks that visibility against your competitors and helps you close the gaps.
